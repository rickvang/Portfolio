# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are prospective clients, employers, and collaborators evaluating Rick Vang's product design practice and deciding whether the work is relevant to their needs.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio communicates how Rick Vang works across product strategy, systems design, design foundations, and AI-assisted delivery. It helps readers understand the decisions behind the work and reach source-backed case studies, notes, and contact information.

## Positioning

The practice connects fragmented product workflows, reusable foundations, and implementation decisions so complex software becomes easier to understand, build, and evolve.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the portfolio on desktop and mobile, often entering through the homepage and moving between selected work, notes, about, and contact routes. The portfolio is reviewed as a working professional presence rather than as an application with authenticated user workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes include Home, Work, Notes, About, Contact, and case-study pages.
- Project presentation must use authentic, source-backed content and approved project media.
- The site should remain readable, keyboard-accessible, responsive, and usable with reduced motion.
- The development harness and any synthetic fixtures are local-only and must not appear in production.
- Claims, outcomes, and project details must not be invented to complete a visual composition.

## Brand Commitments

- The public identity is Rick Vang, product designer.
- The voice should be direct, specific, systems-oriented, and grounded in real work.
- The portfolio should present the work as a connected practice rather than as disconnected visual samples.

## Evidence on Hand

- Source-controlled imported profile and case-study content under `content/imports/`.
- Approved project media under `public/work-media/`.
- Published notes resolved through the repository's publication gate.
- The current working visual and interaction contract in `DESIGN.md`.

## Product Principles

- Put real work before biography or decoration.
- Make relationships between systems, decisions, and outcomes legible.
- Prefer specific evidence over generic positioning language.
- Keep interaction expressive but quiet enough that it does not compete with the work.
- Preserve progressive enhancement and accessible alternatives for visual effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must retain semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, usable touch targets, and reduced-motion behavior. Decorative graphics must not carry essential meaning or replace text content.
